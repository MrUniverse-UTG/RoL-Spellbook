Study of Illusion

Bend light and perception to deceive, disguise, and disappear from the world's sight.

12 spells

Lesser Illusion

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range30 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH

Target: Upon casting, select a point within range that you can see.

Effect: You create a sound or image within range that lasts for the duration.

Sound: This can be a noise or voice you’ve heard before. If a voice, it can only speak one sentence. You can time it to activate at any point during the duration.

Intangible Image: This can be of an object, such as a chair or footprints, that does not take up space bigger than a 5-ft. cube. The image does not produce any other sensory effect, and physically interacting with it makes it transparent and distorted.

Cast Limitation: You can only have one of these effects active per cast.

Silence: You can dispel these effects at any point as a Free action.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: You increase the range to 60 ft.

Rank 4: The duration increases to 10 minutes.

Rank 5: You can use both effects per cast.

Greater Illusion

Rank 1
Power Level0
Range60 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Hour
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Solid Light: Upon casting, you create a solid light illusion of a creature or object at a point you can see within range.

Size: The illusion can be no bigger than a 5-ft. cube.

Sound: You can speak through the illusion.

Animating: You can use a Standard action to move the illusion up to 30 ft. If it is a creature, you can animate its movement.

Properties: The illusion has a Natural DP of 10 and reacts to taking damage. If its DP is brought to 0, the illusion is dispelled.

Limitations: The illusion can’t change temperature or give off heat, nor can it push, lift, or carry any objects that weigh more than 5 lbs.

False Imagery: A suspicious creature within 5 ft. of you can use a Standard action to make an Insight check, penalized by a -1 modifier for every 2 Power Levels of the Spell. On a success, they know it’s an illusion as they see a faint glowing aura coming from it.

Disguised Domain

Rank 5
Power LevelNone
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration8 Hours
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Effect: Upon casting, you create up to a 30-ft. cube within range that you can see. The cube is invisible and the area within seems normal from the outside.

Intangible Terrain: You can completely change the entire area within the cube in the following ways:

  • You can make existing terrain and objects disappear and create new terrain and objects.
  • You can change the temperature, but not enough to harm a creature.
  • You can snuff out and create light.
  • You can hide or create sounds.

Hypnotic: A creature that enters the illusion is put in a hypnotic trance. They can interact with and feel the physical sensations and temperature of the illusion, but their real body just stands still, staring into the distance. They can’t leave the trance regardless of how far they travel while in the illusion. If they would travel beyond the illusion’s boundaries, they are redirected back into the illusion.

Shared Trance: Creatures within the same trance can see and communicate with each other. They can physically interact with and feel each other inside the trance, but deal no damage to their real bodies.

Your Domain: When you cast the Spell, you are not affected by the trance and can choose to be visible or not while within the cube. You can also communicate with creatures in your hypnotic trance. If you do so while not making yourself present within the illusion, you speak to them as a telepathic voice.

False Imagery: A suspicious creature within the Spell’s cube can use a Standard action to make an Insight check. This can not be Assisted.

  • On a success, they know that something is wrong and can find their way out of the cube to break out of the trance. They will be put back in a trance if they enter the cube again.
  • On a critical success, they can see and move through the illusion and can no longer be put in a trance by it.
  • Once a creature has attempted the check, they can’t do so again until 10 minutes have passed or they are physically removed from the illusion.

Snap Back: If a creature takes damage, is harmed, or is pulled out of the illusion with while they are in the trance, then they break out of the trance. If they are still inside the cube, they exit the illusion the way the came.
