Study of Shadows

Bend dark energies to sneak around and quickly incapacitate the weak-minded.

12 spells

Feed Fear

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range15 ft.
Cast TimeQuick
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH

Target: Cast on a creature you can see within range.

Effect: Until the end of your next turn, the next time the target takes Fear damage, they take an additional 2 Fear damage.

Subtle: The hand components used in the cast of this spell are incredibly subtle. Creatures watching you are unaware that you are casting a Spell unless they can cast this Spell.

Numbed Mind: A creature that has been targeted by this spell can’t be targeted by it again until the end of its next turn.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The next time the target takes Fear damage, they now take an additional 4 Fear damage.

Rank 4: The next time the target takes Fear damage, they take an additional 6 Fear damage.

Rank 5: The next time the target takes Fear damage, they take an additional 8 Fear damage.

Manipulate Darkness

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range30 ft.
Cast TimeStandard
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Upon casting, select a 5-ft. square at a point within range.

Effect: You create up to a 5-ft. cube of darkness at that point. The darkness snuffs out and blocks natural light. The darkness is destroyed by Divine Light.

Manipulate: You can use a Quick action to manipulate the darkness in the following ways:

  • Move the darkness up to 30 ft.
  • Have the darkness follow a creature.
  • Change the shape of the darkness to look like the shadow of a creature or object.
  • Materialize the darkness, or return it to its original form.

Materialized: When materialized, the darkness is a solid object with a Natural DP of 10 that weighs 5 lbs. While materialized, you cannot manipulate it until you return it to its normal darkness form.

Limitations: You can only have up to 2 casts of this Spell active at once. If you cast this Spell again while having 2 different casts active, the oldest is dispelled.

Dark Duration: The darkness lasts for 1 minute. Return to

Shadows: You can dispel the darkness at any point as a Free action.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The darkness you summon can now be up to a 10-ft. cube.

Rank 4: You can have up to 3 casts of this Spell active at once.

Rank 5: You can have up to 4 casts of this Spell active at once.

Terrifying Gloom

Rank 1
Power LevelNone
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard
Duration10 Min.
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, create a 15-ft.-radius sphere of Shadow Gloom centered on a point you see within range.

Shadow Gloom: Shadow Gloom is magical darkness. It consumes all natural light. Divine Light does not destroy it but does suppress it. Creatures with Nightvision cannot see through Shadow Gloom.

Terrifying: Creatures that can’t see through Shadow Gloom and start their turn within it take 3 Fear damage.

Roaming: You can use a Quick action to move the Shadow Gloom up to 15 ft. in a straight line.

Merging: If you cast this Spell or move the Shadow Gloom at least 5 ft. into a larger area of Shadow Gloom that already exists, it is sucked into the larger area and merges with it for the duration of the Spell. While merged, the whole area of Shadow Gloom gains the Terrifying property of this Spell, but you can no longer use the Roaming property.

Dark Domain

Rank 5
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeStandard
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, you create a 60-ft.-radius sphere of Shadow Gloom centered on you that wraps around corners.
