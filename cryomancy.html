Study of Cryomancy

Harness the powers of water and cold to freeze, shatter, and encase your enemies.

12 spells

Sculpt Ice

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range20 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Create Ice: Upon casting, create a 5-ft. or 10-ft. cube of ice at a point you can see within range.

Freeze Water: You can instead cast this on normal water that can fill a 5-ft. or 10-ft. cube. The water comes together and forms an ice cube.

Empty Cube: The cube cannot be formed if a creature or object is occupying the space it is forming in.

Ice Durability: Each cube has 10 Natural DP and has immunity to Frost damage.

Limitations: You can only create up to 2 cubes of ice. If you create another one while already having 2, the oldest one evaporates. If you used this Spell on existing water to freeze it, the resulting cube does not count against the max number of cubes you can create.

Sculptures: You can use a Quick action to change the shape of the cube into other simple shapes.

Melting: The ice lasts 1 minute before melting. This can be sped up or extended based on the temperature of the water or environment.

Quick Melt: You can dispel the ice at any point as a Free action.

Ice Barrier

Rank 1
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeReaction
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Reaction: You can cast this Spell when you take Slash, Pierce, Blunt, or Frost damage.

Effect: You reduce the amount of Slash, Pierce, Blunt, or Frost damage you take by 6. If you take damage from more than one of these types simultaneously, the Spell can only block a total of 6 damage. You choose which damage types are blocked and for how much.

Limitation: This Spell can’t reduce the damage taken by more than half its original amount.

Upcast Bonus Effect: For each Rank you cast this at higher than Rank 1, the damage this Spell reduces increases by 6.

Daggers of Ice

Rank 1
Power Level3
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on any number of creatures that you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Effect: The Spell creates a number of ice daggers equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell. You hurl the daggers at the target creature. Each dagger deals 1 Frost damage.

Split Damage: When you are targeting more than one creature, you must decide how many daggers hit which target.

Skill Save: Each target can make a Move save to reduce damage from this Spell. All the daggers thrown at the target count together as one hit.

Upcast Bonus Effect: For each Rank you cast this at higher than Rank 1, it gains an additional Power Level.

Flash Freeze

Rank 3
Power Level6
Range90 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Shape: You create a 30-ft. cube of energy at a point you can see within range that freezes all surfaces within it.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Effect: All surfaces that were in the cube become Slippery for 1 minute. Creatures that were in the cube when you cast this Spell take Frost damage equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell.

Skill Save: Creatures that take damage from this Spell can make an Endure save to reduce the damage.

Slippery: When a creature moves 5 ft. onto a Slippery surface for the first time since the end of their last turn, they must make a Move save. On a failed save they fall Prone. On a successful save, they can move but treat it as a difficult surface.

Chilled Water: Water within the cube of this Spell does not become frozen, but creatures in that water gain a -2 modifier to their Endure save against this Spell.

Cryostasis

Rank 3
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeReaction
DurationInstant
Focusno
ComponentsH, T

Trigger: When you are about to take physical damage, you can cast this Spell.

Effect: You become Frozen and encased in ice until the end of your next turn. While Frozen, you gain immunity to all physical damage.

Side Effects: While Frozen, you are also Dazed, you can’t move or perform any actions, and you automatically fail Strength and Agility saves.

NPC Limitations: If an Elite or Mythic creature casts this Spell, they remain Frozen until the end of the turn after their next turn. This means they are unable to act on their next turn, and only at the end of their following turn do they unfreeze.

Frozen Location: If you are touching a surface when you become Frozen by this Spell, you can’t be moved from that spot except by forces greater than 5,000 lbs. If you are flying or not on the surface, you fall once Frozen. Creatures your Frozen, falling body lands on take 30 Blunt damage plus an additional 10 Blunt damage for every size you are bigger than Medium.

Skill Save: A creature that would take damage from you landing on them can make a Move save, dodging and avoiding the damage on a success.

Boreal Binds

Rank 4
Power Level4
RangeSelf
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Upon casting, choose up to three creatures you can see within 30 ft. of you.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Effect: Each creature that you targeted takes 12 Frost damage.

Skill Save: Each target also makes a Move save, penalized by a -1 modifier for every 2 Power Levels of the Spell.

Failed Save Bonus Effect: On a failed save, targets take an additional 12 Frost damage and become Restrained by ice chains for the duration.

Shorter Duration: A creature Restrained by this Spell can use a Standard action to make a Might check. On a success, they break free from the chains. If no creatures are Restrained by this Spell, it ends.

Unstable Shard

Rank 4
Power Level12
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on a creature that you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

FocusYes

Avalanche

Rank 5
Power Level10
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, choose a point you can see within range. You create up to a 50-ft. cube of heavy snow that crashes centered at the point you chose.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level to 10 + the number of successes on the Spell check.

Effect: Each creature in the area where the snow crashes takes Frost damage equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell.

Skill Save: Each creature that takes damage from this Spell can make a Might save to reduce damage.

Buried: A Large or smaller creature also becomes Buried. A Buried creature is Restrained, Dazed, and Prone but gains Full Cover from everything.

Dig Out: A Buried creature can make a Might check as a Quick action to try and dig to end the Condition early. Another creature can use a Standard action to make that Might check for a Buried creature.

Hill: The Spell leaves a 15-ft.-tall hill of snow where it landed for the duration.

Sculptures: After the snow lands, you can choose one of the following effects: -

Heavy Snow: Any Might check to try and dig out a Buried creature gains a -2 modifier. -

Freezing Snow: The Spell deals 10 Frost damage to a creature it has Buried at the end of their turns.
