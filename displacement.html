Study of Displacement

Specialize in teleportation and spatial manipulation to reposition allies and confound enemies.

12 spells

Pocketswap

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range5 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH

Target: Cast on a bag, pocket, or container you can see within range.

Pickpocket: You attempt to swap the contents of the target container with the contents of a container, bag, or pocket you are holding or wearing. Make a Finesse check using your Spellcasting Attribute. On a success the contents are swapped, each teleporting to the other container.

Not Always Easy: The GM may impose a Difficulty based on the Insight of the creature the pocket is on or other magical effects.

Oopsie: If you fail the Finesse check, the contents are teleported between the two containers and fall to the ground. All creatures within sight are alerted to it.

Subtle: the Hand components of this Spell are very small and not easily seen, allowing you to cast this without being noticed.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The Spell’s range increases to 10 ft.

Rank 4: The Spell’s range increases to 20 ft.

Rank 5: The Spell’s range increases to 30 ft.

Infini Pocket

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
RangeTouch
Cast TimeQuick Action
Duration1 Week
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on a bag, pocket, or container with an opening no bigger than 1 ft. in diameter. Bigger on the

Inside: You enchant the item to become a portal to a personal pocket realm. You may use a Minor action to store items that can fit through the opening of the bag or pocket into the pocket realm.

Pocket Realm: The pocket realm can store up to 2 Tiny items. If you try to add more than that, the container acting as a portal breaks and all the items fall out. There is no air within the pocket realm.

Lighter: Whenever there is an item stored in the pocket realm, the enchanted bag or pocket feels like it is holding something but does not change in size and only weighs 2 lbs.

Extended Duration: If you cast this Spell on a container that is already under the effects of your Spell, it refreshes the duration.

Only One: There is only one personal pocket realm per caster. If this Spell is cast again on a different bag, the last portal closes and the new portal leads to the same pocket realm.

Collapse: If you try to store an object with an active portal on it in the pocket realm, the realm collapses and spills out all items it was containing.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The pocket realm can hold up to 4 Tiny items.

Rank 4: The pocket realm can hold up to 6 Tiny items.

Rank 5: The pocket realm can hold up to 8 Tiny items.

Minor Portals

Rank 1
Power LevelNone
Range30 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Summon: Upon casting, create two linked portals on surfaces you can see within range. Each portal must be cast on a 1-ft.-diameter solid surface, may not be touching a creature, and may not be touching another portal.

Portal: The portals are 1 ft. in diameter. You can see and reach through the portals as if they were open windows to each other.

Stationary: The portals cannot be moved after they are summoned.

Arrow Slit: You can make melee or ranged attacks through a portal. Targeting through the portal while being more than 5 ft. from it counts as the target having Heavy Cover.

Ouch: If a creature’s body part is sticking through the portal when the Spell ends, the creature takes 4 Force damage and is pushed to one side of the portal. The side they are pushed to is the one they are mostly on.

Major Portals

Rank 4
Power LevelNone
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeQuick Action
Duration24 hours
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Anchored Surface: Upon cast, you designate a solid, unmoving surface you can see within range. This surface must measure at least 3-ft. in diameter. This initial location becomes Anchored by the spell. An invisible sigil or other magical marker (your choice) appears on it for the spell’s duration. The anchored surface cannot already have an active portal of any kind.

Linked Surface: Until the spell ends, you may use a Quick Action to select a second solid surface you can see within range (but can be any distance away from the Anchored Surface) that also measures at least 3-ft. in diameter and does not already have an active portal effect upon it. This second surface becomes the Linked Surface.

Summon: When you choose the second surface, a matching portal manifests on both the Anchored Surface and the Linked Surface. Each portal expands up to 5 ft. in diameter, or as large as the available surface allows, whichever is smaller.

Portal: You can see, move, and act through the portals as if they were open doorways to each other. The gravity and physics of the portals are maintained on their respective ends.

Size Limitations: Only Medium or smaller creatures can fit through the portals.

Stationary: The portals cannot be moved after they are summoned, but the surface they are on can be moved if it is on a movable object.

Ouch: If a creature’s body part is sticking through the portal when the Spell ends, the creature takes 12 Force damage and is pushed to one side of the portal (their choice).

Begone: If you summon one of the portals beneath a Medium or smaller creature, they must make a Move save. On a failed save, they fall through and get spit out 10 ft. from the other portal.

Shroud Sanctum

Rank 5
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Effect: You create a Personal Realm in the Shroud and open a connection to your current location. When you cast this spell, you can choose to be teleported to it or stay in your location. When you teleport to the refuge, you take any Bulky or smaller items you are carrying with you. A glowing sigil appears where you were, marking your Anchor Point for this spell for the duration while away.

Personal Realm: This is a 20-ft. cube pocket of anchored space tied to your soul. Your refuge is a small, stable chamber suspended within the Shroud. Its appearance reflects your nature. Inside this space: - Time flows at the rate your Anchor Point is at. - You can act and move freely within the chamber. - You cannot see, contact, or interact with the original location you came from. - You can’t create portals here.

Achor Point: You may use a Minor Action to return to the original location you came from. When you do, you appear at the Anchor Point, in the exact orientation you left. You can also use a Minor Action to teleport back into your Personal Realm, creating a new Anchor point if you move to a new location.

Forced Out: A creature within 5 ft. of the Anchor Point can use a Standard Action to make a Might check to attempt to pull the caster out. The caster may oppose this check with an Endure save. On a success, the Caster is forced to teleport back to the Anchor Point.

Not Permanent: When the spell ends, you and any items you brought into the Personal Realm are returned to the Anchor Point as the Personal Realm collapses.
