Study of Pyromancy

Harness Sorce offensively through fire — the best defense is an overwhelming offense.

12 spells

Dancing Flame

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range30 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Hour
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Conjuration: Upon casting, you create a small, harmless flame that provides 30 ft. of light. The light floats around you.

Mobile Flame: You can use a Quick action to have the flame move and float around another creature within range. If the creature moves out of range, the flame reappears and follows you.

Snuff: You can dispel these effects at any point as a Free action.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The range of the Spell increases to 60 ft.

Rank 4: The light the flame provides increases to 60 ft.

Rank 5: The range of the Spell and the light the flame provides increases to 90 ft.

Wall of Fire

Rank 4
Power Level8
Range90 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, this Spell creates a 60-ft. line of fire, centered at a point on a surface that you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Effect: The line creates a 15-ft.-tall wall of fire that extends from the surface. This wall provides 20 ft. of light and Heavy Cover.

Hazard: A creature that enters or is forced into the wall for the first time since the start of their last turn takes Fire damage equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell.

Skill Save: Creatures that take damage from this Spell can make a Move save to reduce damage.

Upcast Bonus Effect: If you cast this at Rank 5, you can bend the wall. This allows you to make the wall a 15-ft.-radius circle instead of a 60-ft. line. Creatures that end their turn in the circle take Fire damage equal to the Power Level of the Spell. This does not affect creatures that are higher than 15 ft. above the circle.
