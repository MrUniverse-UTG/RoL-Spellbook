Study of Divinity

Call upon the blessings of divine power to smite enemies and shield the faithful.

12 spells

Blessing of Protection

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range10 ft.
Cast TimeReaction
DurationInstant
FocusYes
ComponentsT, H

Trigger: When a creature you see takes damage, you can cast this Spell on them.

Effect: You bless the target with protection. The damage the target takes is reduced by 4 to a minimum of 1.

Shorter Duration: Once this Spell’s blessing has been used, the Spell ends.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: The Spell reduces the damage by 6.

Rank 4: The Spell reduces the damage by 8.

Rank 5: The Spell reduces the damage by 10.

Vengeful Smite

Rank 1
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeQuick Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsT

Smite: While this Spell is active, your next Melee Weapon Attack or Unarmed Strike that hits deals an additional 4 Radiant damage.

Multitarget: If this Spell is used with an ability or attack that targets multiple creatures, or states “As if you hit with a weapon attack,” it is only used against the first creature hit by that ability or attack.

Vengeful: If the creature that you hit with this Spell’s additional damage attacked or damaged you since the end of your last turn, the smite deals an additional 8 Radiant damage instead.

Shorter Duration: Once this Spell’s additional damage has been dealt, the Spell ends.

Blessing of Pacifism

Rank 1
Power LevelNone
Range15 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast upon a creature you can see within range.

Blessing: You bless the target with pacifism. Whenever a creature targets the blessed creature with an attack or Spell, the attacker must make an Insight save. On a failed save, the attacker must target another creature within the same range or waste their action.

Hit But Protected: If the attacker succeeds on the save and the attack goes through, the blessed creature gains the effects of Blessing of Protection against that attack as if you casted it. You do not need to have Blessing of Protection prepared for this.

Collateral Damage: A creature that inflicts damage in an area of effect does not have to make an Insight save if the blessed creature would be in the affected area, so long as they don’t specifically target the blessed creature. If the blessed creature is harmed by the area, they still gain the effects of Blessing of Protection.

Shorter Duration: If a creature blessed by this Spell attacks, casts a Spell that deals damage, casts a Spell that inflicts a Condition, Taunts, Heckles, or harms another creature in any way, the Spell ends early.

Blessing of Radiance

Rank 3
Power LevelNone
Range60 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast upon a creature you can see within range.

Blessing: You bless the target with the blinding radiance of Sol. The blessed creature emits 30 ft. of Divine Light.

Divine Light: Light created by this Spell suppresses Shadow Gloom and counts as sunlight. Any weapon attacks made within this Divine Light is treated as if they are Silvered.

Radiance: A creature that starts their turn within 5 ft. of a creature blessed by this Spell must choose whether they will look away or not until the start of their next turn.

Look Away: If a creature looks away, the blessed creature gains the Obscured Condition against that creature.

My Eyes: If a creature does not look away, they must make an Endure save. On a failed save, they gain the Blinded Condition until the start of their next turn. On a successful save, nothing happens.

Immunities: Creatures that have Blind Vision, Tremor Vision, or Omni Vision are immune to being blinded by this spell.

Limitation: Only one cast of this Spell can be active at once. If you cast this Spell again, the last cast is dispelled.

Divine Sanctuary

Rank 4
Power Level12
RangeSelf
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, a 15-ft.-radius sphere force field expands from you.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Allowed: When you cast this Spell, you can designate any number of creatures you see within range to be Allowed by this Spell.

Divine Dome: Creatures you did not designate as Allowed are pushed back 15 ft. from you when the dome is made. An Allowed creature can use a Minor action to step 5 ft. through the dome to one of its other sides. The dome provides Full Cover to creatures on the opposite side of its walls and blocks any spells or abilities from passing through.

Dome Properties: The dome is a magical wall that has a Natural DP equal to double the Power Level of the Spell. The dome is transparent with a golden hue.

Absorb Damage: If a hazard or Spell would deal physical damage in an area that would destroy the dome and then hit the creatures inside it, the dome halves that damage before it is destroyed.
