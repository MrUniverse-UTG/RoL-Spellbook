Study of Aeromancy

Harness the primal powers of air and lightning to enhance speed, control the field, and blast enemies.

12 spells

Jolt

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
Range15-ft. Line
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, you shoot a 15-ft. line of lightning originating from you.

Skill Save: Each creature in the line must make a Move save.

Effect: On a failed save, the creature takes 6 Electric damage. On a successful save, they dodge and take no damage.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: Move saves against this Spell gain a -2 modifier.

Rank 4: The Electric damage increases to 8.

Rank 5: Move saves against this Spell gain a -4 modifier.

Bellowing Shout

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeQuick Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsT

Skill Save: Upon casting, each creature within 5 ft. of you must make an Endure save.

Effect: On a failed save, the creature takes 6 Force damage as you shout one word with a thundering voice. On a successful save, they take no damage.

Loud: The word you speak can be heard up to a mile away.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: Creatures within 10 ft. must make the save.

Rank 4: Creatures within 15 ft. must make the save.

Rank 5: The Force damage increases by 2.

Lightning Arc

Rank 1
Power Level3
Range40 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on a creature that you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Effect: The target creature takes Electric damage equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell.

Jumping: Once this Spell hits a creature, you can have it jump to a second creature within 20 ft. of the first to deal the same damage.

Skill Save: A creature that takes damage from this Spell can make a Move save to reduce damage.

Upcast Bonus Effect: For each Rank you cast this higher than 1, you can have the lightning jump to an additional creature within 20 ft. It cannot jump to a target that it has already hit.

Power Level6
Range60 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Storm of Fury

Rank 5
Power Level10
RangeSelf
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Shape: Upon casting, create a dark thundercloud that spreads in a 120-ft.-radius circle centered 60 ft. above you. The cloud’s height rises 20 ft. above that.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Open Sky: If the thundercloud can’t be summoned 60 ft. high, the Spell fails.

Thundercloud: This cloud obscures line of sight and sparks with electricity. While this Spell is active, you can use a Quick action to have the cloud strike lighting at a target creature you can see under the cloud. It can’t strike creatures behind Full Cover.

Zap: A creature struck by lightning from the thundercloud takes Electric damage equal to triple the Power Level of the Spell. If a creature is hit by more than one lighting strike before the start of your next turn, the additional strikes only deal Lighting damage equal to the Power Level of the Spell.

Skill Save: A creature that takes damage from this Spell can make a Move save to reduce damage.

Roaming Storm: You can use a Standard action to move the thundercloud up to 30 ft. It must remain 60 ft. above the ground.

Magnetivus

Rank 5
Power Level5
Range120 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Shape: When you cast this Spell, create a 30-ft.-radius sphere centered on a point you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level of the Spell.

Skill Save: When a creature starts their turn in the sphere or is moved into it for the first time since the start of their last turn, they take 15 Force damage and must make a Might save. They receive a -1 modifier for every 2 Power Levels of the Spell.

Effect: On a failed save, the creature is pulled as close to the center of the sphere as they can get and is Restrained until the start of their next turn.

Homing: Ranged attacks gain a +2 modifier when targeting a creature Restrained by this Spell so long as the creature is not benefiting from Cover against the attack.

Push Through: On a successful save, the creature is not pulled. All movement within the sphere costs double the Speed.
