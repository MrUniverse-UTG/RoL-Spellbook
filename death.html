Reaper Scythe

Rank 0
Power LevelNone
RangeSelf
Cast TimeQuick Action
Duration1 Minute
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Effect: You create a dark reaper scythe in your hands. The reaper scythe lasts for 1 minute or until you dispel it.

Reaper Scythe: This weapon is a 1h melee weapon and has a base damage of 4 Slash. When you use this weapon to attack or Parry, you can use your Spellcasting Attribute instead of Strength for the Skill check.

Executioner: The reaper scythe deals 4 additional Necrotic damage if the target of the attack is Bloodied.

Attuned: If you let go or are disarmed of the scythe, it is dispelled.

Enhanced Study

When you gain certain Ranks in this Study, you gain additional benefits.

Rank 3: Increase the base damage of the scythe by 2.

Rank 4: The scythe increases in size, can be used with 2h, and gains the Versatile Feature.

Raise Dead

Rank 3
Power LevelNone
Range60 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
Duration8 Hours
FocusYes
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on the corpse of a Large or smaller creature that you can see within range.

Effect: When you cast this Spell, choose to either make the corpse a Raised Undead or Revived Undead. You can only choose Revived Undead if the corpse has not been dead for longer than 1 minute.

Raised Undead: You reanimate the corpse into a mindless Undead that follows your commands. Refer to the Raised Undead stat block for its stats and actions. The Raised Undead is treated as a Summoned Companion.

Size Counts: The size of the corpse affects the stats of a Raised Undead. The bigger it is, the stronger it is.

Unchained: If you lose your focus on this Spell, the Raised Undead is not dispelled, but gains the Enraged Condition at the start of each of its turns for the remainder of the duration.

Rebinding: If you are within 60 ft. of a Raised Undead that you lost focus on, you can use a Quick action to attempt to regain control. On a success, you regain focus on this Spell and the Raised Undead is no longer Enraged.

Revived Undead: You briefly return the soul back to the body. They regain all missing HP and MP, but their max HP and max MP are reduced by half. The soul has full control over the body. When you lose focus or the Spell ends, the body crumbles to dust and the soul returns to the Shroud.

Upcast Bonus Effect: If you cast this Spell at Rank 4, the size of the corpse can be Huge. If you cast this Spell at Rank 5, the size of the corpse can be Gargantuan.

Dark Exchange

Rank 5
Power Level4
Range60 ft.
Cast TimeStandard Action
DurationInstant
FocusNo
ComponentsH, T

Target: Cast on a creature you can see within range.

Spell Check: You can perform a Spell check upon casting to increase the Power Level to 5 + the number of successes.

Effect: You Curse the creature with Dark Exchange, creating ghostly chains that bind their soul to your body. The Curse lasts until the end of your next turn.

Dark Exchange: While the Curse is active: - Whenever your HP or DP is reduced, the Cursed creature takes Necrotic damage equal to the amount of HP or DP you lost. - The Cursed creature has all their Speeds reduced by 15 ft. and you gain 15 ft. of Speed.
